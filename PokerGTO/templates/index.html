Why think in ranges?

Great players make decisions based on the full set of hands they could hold, not a single combo. Reviewing ranges helps you build balanced strategies that are harder to exploit.

Table position matters

Positions like the Button (BTN) and Cutoff (CO) can open wider. Early positions should stay tighter to avoid tricky multiway pots.
