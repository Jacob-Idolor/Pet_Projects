## Design Suggestions for Icon:

1. **Playing Cards Design:**
   - Two cards (Ace and King)
   - Gradient background (#00ff87 to #60efff)
   - Dark background (#1a1a2e)
   - "GTO" text subtly incorporated

2. **Minimalist:**
   - Just "GTO" letters
   - Poker chip or card suit
   - Clean and modern

3. **Badge Style:**
   - Circular badge
   - Crown or trophy icon
   - Numbers/stats theme

## Tools to Create Icons:

1. **Figma** - Design at 1024x1024, export all sizes
2. **App Icon Generator** - https://appicon.co
3. **Canva** - Easy templates
4. **Adobe Illustrator** - Professional vector design
