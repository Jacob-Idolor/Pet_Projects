## Sample 1: one evidence record

Copy the block below for each material claim. Bracketed text is a prompt, not data.

### C-01 — [Short claim]

- Evidence type: [reported result / management guidance / provider estimate / calculation / assumption]
- Value or statement: [exact value or careful paraphrase]
- Unit and currency: [for example USD millions]
- Period or as-of date: [exact dates]
- Source: [S-xx, document title, source URL and exact section]
- Retrieved at: [date and time zone]
- Definition and exclusions: [what the metric includes and leaves out]
- Calculation, if any: [inputs with C-IDs, formula and rounding]
- Verification: [not checked / verified against source / conflicting / superseded]
- What this does NOT establish: [inference the source cannot support]
- Related assumption or contradiction: [A-xx / X-xx / none]

## Sample 2: a completed fictional record

**FICTIONAL TEACHING EXAMPLE.** Example Compute Co. and all numbers below are
invented. They do not describe NBIS, another real company or current market data.

Fictional source S-01, "Q2 operating update," Section A:

> Revenue for the three months ended June 30, 2026 was USD 18 million, compared
> with USD 12 million for the three months ended June 30, 2025. Both figures cover
> the consolidated business and use the same revenue definition.

### C-01 — Revenue rose from USD 12 million to USD 18 million

- Evidence type: reported result within the fictional exhibit above.
- Unit: USD millions; consolidated business.
- Periods: April 1–June 30, 2026 and April 1–June 30, 2025.
- Source: fictional S-01, Section A, reproduced in this sample; no external URL.
- Verification: checked against this fictional teaching text only.
- Limitation: the source does not establish what caused the increase.

### C-02 — Calculated growth was 50%

- Evidence type: calculation from C-01.
- Absolute change: 18 − 12 = USD 6 million.
- Relative change: (18 − 12) / 12 × 100 = 50%.
- Limitation: a historical comparison in a fictional exercise, not a forecast.

## A useful follow-up question

"What source evidence would distinguish customer growth, pricing and service mix
as explanations for this change?"

Until that evidence is available, do not label any one explanation as proven.
The complete worked example adds a capacity disclosure, a later correction,
contradiction handling and a short memo with a deliberately unresolved causal question.
