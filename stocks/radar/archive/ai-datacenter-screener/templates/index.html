📈 Score Backtest — is the score predictive?

Reconstructs daily close-of-day scores over the lookback, then checks whether higher scores preceded higher forward returns. Price-driven metrics (multiples, technicals, upside) are recomputed each day from real historical closes; slow fundamentals (margins, growth, debt…) are held at today's values — there's no free point-in-time fundamental data — so this is a relative signal test, not a trading simulation.
