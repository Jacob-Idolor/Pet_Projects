# StocksWatch income MVP — September 2026

## Decision and scope

Continue with StocksWatch and its NBIS research desk. The broader AI/automation brief is the long-term direction, not a reason to replace the working application or launch five unrelated content pillars now. This is a proposed commercial experiment, not validated demand or an income forecast.

This document develops the twelve planning deliverables from Jacob's brief. It moves a small, reusable digital product ahead of sponsorship and a paid archive. A recurring research memo creates an editorial obligation; an evergreen workflow product better fits the intended maintenance budget. Keep Astro, TypeScript, static output and Cloudflare Pages. No framework migration is necessary for this phase.

## 1. Positioning

**Understand AI infrastructure. Build a repeatable research process.**

StocksWatch helps technically minded readers organize company disclosures, cloud economics, assumptions and evidence gaps. NBIS is the first worked research desk. The value proposition is clarity and an organized process, never an investing edge or promised returns.

Homepage remains the NBIS desk. The new research-kit landing page says: **A clearer process for AI infrastructure research.** Primary action: download the free kit. Secondary action: see what is inside. Keep the kit available without an email while no provider is connected.

## 3. Ideal first customer

A working engineer, cloud operator or technically curious professional who follows AI infrastructure and already reads company disclosures, but has no consistent system for recording sources and checking assumptions. They want reusable files they can own and adapt. Do not simultaneously target active traders, beginner investors and every small-business owner.

## 4. Primary problem

Research is scattered across filings, dashboards, headlines and personal notes. Readers struggle to separate a reported fact from a provider estimate or their own assumption, and to remember what changed. The first kit supplies a repeatable source log, assumption register and review checklist.

## 5. Monetization hierarchy

1. Free desk, useful guides and an ungated research kit establish usefulness.
2. Optional consent-based email builds a relationship once a provider is configured.
3. Test a **$19 one-time Research Workflow Pack**: editable evidence log, assumption register, review checklist, a fictional worked example and a short usage guide. Price is a hypothesis. No preorder or payment collection before delivery is ready.
4. Add a complementary technical template only if readers request it.
5. Consider disclosed affiliate recommendations and one sponsor after verified audience measurement exists. No paid ranking or invented first-hand experience.
6. Consider a paid archive only after repeated requests, a data-retention plan and redistribution-rights review. Do not sell copied provider feeds by default.

Ads remain supplemental. Do not add subscriptions, authentication or premium market data merely to appear monetized.

## 9. Five free tools worth testing

1. **Research checklist builder:** select a research question and export an editable checklist. No accounts.
2. **Source freshness checker:** enter an observation date and review interval; flag when to recheck. Browser-only, no claim that fresh means correct.
3. **Assumption sensitivity explorer:** user-entered educational scenarios, explicit units and assumptions, no price targets.
4. **Automation time calculator:** compare setup plus maintenance time with repetitive manual work. Show break-even under user-entered assumptions.
5. **Cloud workload cost worksheet:** user-supplied rates and usage, explicit exclusions and units. No hardcoded claim of current vendor pricing.

Begin with the checklist, already useful as a downloadable file. Add interactive behavior only when it improves a demonstrated task.

## 10. Recommended MVP and acceptance criteria

### This increment

- Preserve the research homepage and its fresh/stale data behavior.
- Add a responsive kit page with a preview, editable Markdown download and reading-guide links.
- Clearly state that the planned paid pack is unavailable; no checkout or fake signup.
- Add navigation, canonical metadata, sitemap inclusion and release verification.
- Keep the existing fonts, theme control and accessibility conventions.

### Complete commercial MVP after validation

- About page with accurate, user-supplied experience; three new cornerstone guides.
- Select one email provider; configure a verified sender, consent, unsubscribe, privacy copy and a tested confirmation flow. Default to occasional evergreen updates, not a promised daily newsletter.
- Connect minimal aggregate measurement. Existing browser events alone do not store analytics. Track kit-page visits, downloads, confirmed opt-ins and eventual purchases without sending email addresses in analytics payloads.
- Finish the $19 pack, preview and license; select one hosted checkout with automated delivery. Test purchase, receipt, download, duplicate webhook handling if relevant and refund process before launch.
- Document support boundaries: file usage and errors, not personalized research or financial advice. No lifetime-update promise.

Do not claim the email, checkout, analytics or paid product is active until verified end to end.

Suggested internal gates (not industry benchmarks): review after 100 kit downloads or 90 days, whichever comes first. Seek at least five substantive feedback responses before building a second product. After the first ten paying customers, examine delivery issues, refunds and support time before expanding. If traffic is too low, improve distribution and positioning rather than treating an absent sale as product validation.

## 12. Make the business increasingly semi-passive

Aim for a maintenance budget of three focused hours a week after setup; measure it and reduce scope if it is exceeded. This is a design constraint, not a promise that the business needs only three hours.

- Automate snapshot validation, build checks and bounded retries. Investigate failures; never substitute invented fresh data.
- Reuse one source-backed checklist across guide, download and product documentation.
- Let a selected provider handle consent, unsubscribe and transactional delivery.
- Batch editorial review monthly; keep a quarterly link/source review and product version log.
- Provide file-format help, a sample, a FAQ and bounded support before adding more products.
- Review downloads, opt-ins, purchases, refunds, costs and maintenance minutes monthly.
- Pause products that require recurring custom work. Avoid daily social posting and a paid memo publishing obligation in the MVP.

Unit-economics worksheet: monthly gross = orders × price; contribution = gross − payment/platform fees − refunds − hosting/email/data costs. Divide contribution by actual owner hours to assess the time tradeoff. No earnings projection is justified until these inputs are observed.
